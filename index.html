Compatibility

Who may receive the blessing.

ChristPlug works miracles, but it works them on what is already there. Check all three before you take the vow.

I.

Your car

The car’s original screen must already support wired CarPlay or Android Auto from the factory.

II.

Your iPhone

For CarPlay: iPhone 6 or later, running iOS 10 or above.

III.

Your Android

For Android Auto: an Android phone running Android 11 or above.

A note of humility: ChristPlug cannot give CarPlay or Android Auto to a car that never had it. It only supports the original car screen, so aftermarket screens and cars without factory wired CarPlay / Android Auto are beyond even its powers.

Questions of faith

Frequently asked questions.

Will ChristPlug work in my car?

If your car’s original screen already supports wired CarPlay or Android Auto from the factory, yes. If it doesn’t, no amount of prayer will help. It only supports the original car screen.

Which phones are welcome?

For CarPlay, iPhone 6 and later on iOS 10 or above. For Android Auto, Android phones on Android 11 and above. All are welcome. Some just need an update first.

How do I set it up?

Plug it into your car’s USB port (Type-C cars use the included adapter). Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on your phone, find the Bluetooth name shown on the car screen, tap Connect and agree to use CarPlay or Android Auto. From then on it connects by itself when you start the car.

Is it fast?

One independent reviewer measured 6.98 seconds to connect an iPhone and 11.06 seconds for Android, after a 10-second boot. Quicker than most sermons.

Can I still use my steering wheel buttons?

Yes. Steering wheel controls, buttons and knobs keep working as they always have. Thou shalt not relearn.

Does it still work with a cable?

Yes. It retains the wired connection function, for days when you’d rather do penance.

How does it get updates?

Through OTA online upgrades. Open the upgrade URL in your phone’s browser to update, send feedback or change settings. Keep the power steady while it updates. It restarts by itself when it’s done.

What do the lights mean?

Light blue means it has power. Dark green means CarPlay is connected. Dark blue means Android Auto is connected. Signs and wonders, colour-coded.
